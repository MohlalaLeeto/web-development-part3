History of Mohlala's Workshop

The business was founded in 2015 by Mr Mohlala Leeto after he finished his diploma in motor mechanics engineering from Tshwane University of Technology. He started by fixing vehicles at his place as a way to make a living, specialising in any mechanical problem a vehicle can have. He later came up with the idea of building a workshop along with his brother, Mr Mohlala Molefe, after Molefe finished his bachelor's degree in mechatronics engineering from Stellenbosch University in 2018. His brother specialises in auto electrical. They built the workshop in 2020 and it started operating in 2021.

Mission and Vision of the workshop

Mission: To make a living from the skills they gained at tertiary level, and to train and employ people in their community.

Vision: To see the workshop reduce unemployment in the community by training and then employing local people.
